This tool uses AI inpainting to reconstruct the area beneath a selected watermark or unwanted object, blending the result with the surrounding image. Results vary with how complex the background behind the watermark is.

<div class="warn">Only use this on images you own or have explicit rights to edit. Removing a watermark from someone else's copyrighted work without permission may violate copyright law regardless of the tool used.</div>

Select the area covering the watermark or object, run the removal, and review the result before downloading — inpainting occasionally needs a second pass on complex backgrounds.
