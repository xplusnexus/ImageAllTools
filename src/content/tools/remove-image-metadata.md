Most photos carry hidden EXIF metadata — camera model, exact GPS coordinates, timestamp, sometimes even the software used to edit it. Sharing a photo publicly without stripping this can unintentionally reveal your location or device details.

Upload your photo and download a clean copy with all metadata stripped — the visible image is completely unchanged, only the hidden data is removed.
