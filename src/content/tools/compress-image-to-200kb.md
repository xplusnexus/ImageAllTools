200KB is a comfortable target for email attachments, general web forms, and most upload widgets that aren't ID-photo-strict. At this size you can usually keep quality above 85% with no visible compression artifacts at all.

This is also a good target for blog post images if you're not ready to fully resize-and-compress per §5 of a proper website image workflow — a quick single-step compression here still meaningfully helps page speed.
