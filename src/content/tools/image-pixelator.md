Pixelation is the classic "mosaic" privacy technique — dividing an area into large, flat-colored blocks so the underlying detail becomes unrecognizable. Some viewers find it a clearer visual signal of "this was intentionally hidden" than a smooth blur.

Paint over the area to pixelate, adjust the block size for a coarser or finer mosaic, and download. As with the Blur tool, everything runs locally — nothing is ever uploaded.
