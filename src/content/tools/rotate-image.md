Photos taken on a phone sometimes save with incorrect orientation metadata, or you simply need a quick 90° turn before publishing. This tool rotates by fixed 90°/180°/270° increments or any custom angle, individually or in batch.

Upload one or several images, choose landscape-only or portrait-only batch rotation if needed, and download the corrected files.
