Photographers and content creators watermark images to deter unauthorized reuse and to keep attribution visible when photos get shared or re-posted. A good watermark is visible enough to discourage misuse but subtle enough not to distract from the photo itself.

Upload your image, add a text or logo watermark, set its opacity, size, and position (bottom-right is the most common convention), and apply it across single or batch images.
