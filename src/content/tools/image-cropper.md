## Square vs. Circle PFP / Avatar Cropper: Transparent PNG Export

One of the most frequent challenges users face when updating profile pictures (PFPs) across modern messaging apps and gaming platforms is unwanted white or black corner borders.

### The Problem with Standard JPG Cropping
When you crop a circular avatar using basic online tools and export it as a standard JPG, the format does not support transparency. As a result, the tool fills the four corners with solid white or black pixels. When uploaded to a dark-mode app like Discord or Slack, these corners appear as glaring boxes around your round face.

### The ImageAll Circle Avatar Solution
ImageAll features a dedicated **Circle / Round Avatar** shape selector. When activated:
1. An interactive circular boundary with dashed neon alignment guides appears over your photo.
2. The canvas clips the pixels outside the circle with mathematical anti-aliasing.
3. The exported image is saved as a 32-bit **PNG with full alpha-channel transparency** (or modern WEBP).
4. The result is a clean, floating circular badge that seamlessly blends into dark mode, light mode, or custom profile backgrounds.

## Photographic Framing Tips Using the Rule of Thirds

Our interactive cropper displays dynamic **Rule-of-Thirds grid lines** across your crop frame. You can use these professional photography principles to elevate your compositions:

- **Portraits and Headshots**: Align the subject's eye line along the upper horizontal third line. For centered headshots, place the nose bridge at the intersection of the center vertical axis and upper horizontal line.
- **Landscape Photography**: Avoid placing the horizon dead-center in the frame. Position the horizon along the lower third line if the sky contains dramatic clouds, or along the upper third line to emphasize textured foreground terrain.
- **Action and Wildlife**: Position moving subjects on the side of the frame with open space in front of them (the "lead room" principle). This creates a sense of direction and dynamic motion.
- **E-Commerce and Catalog Items**: For clean Amazon or Shopify product cards, switch to the `1:1 Square` preset and click **Center 80% Box** to maintain equal margins around your product.
