## Step-by-Step Tutorial: How to Build a Professional Photo Collage

### Step 1: Upload Your Photos
Click **Click to upload** or drag 2 to 9+ pictures into the dropzone. Supported formats include **JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, BMP, and SVG**. Your images load directly into client browser memory with zero network delay.

### Step 2: Choose Your Grid Template & Ratio
Select your desired template from the **Grid layout** dropdown. Choose **Smart Auto Grid** to let our layout algorithm automatically compute the optimal column and row configuration based on how many photos you have uploaded, or manually lock in a **2-Side**, **3-Hero**, **4-Grid**, or **3×3** format.

### Step 3: Customize Border Spacing, Corner Radius & Frame Color
- **Border Spacing (Gap)**: Slide from 0px (seamless edge-to-edge mosaic) up to 40px (spacious magazine frame).
- **Corner Roundness (Radius)**: Slide from 0px (sharp modern architectural edges) up to 40px (soft, rounded, organic bubbles).
- **Frame Color**: Click our pre-curated designer swatches—**Pure White**, **Dark Slate (#0c0d12)**, **Aesthetic Cream (#fdfbf7)**, **Pastel Pink (#fce7f3)**, or **Warm Beige (#f5ebe0)**—or pick any custom hex shade using the color wheel.

### Step 4: Reorder or Remove Individual Images
Use the interactive thumbnail strip below the controls to inspect all loaded photos. Click the **×** button on any thumbnail to instantly delete a photo and watch the collage rebalance in real time. Click **+ Add More Photos** to queue additional pictures anytime.

### Step 5: Download Instant High-Resolution PNG
Click **Download Result**. Your high-resolution composition is generated instantly via HTML5 Canvas binary blob streaming and saved directly to your Downloads folder—completely unwatermarked and uncompressed.
