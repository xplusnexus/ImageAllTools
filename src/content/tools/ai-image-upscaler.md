## What is an AI Image Upscaler?
**Definition:** An AI image upscaler is a tool that uses deep learning models — such as Super-Resolution Convolutional Neural Networks (SRCNN) or Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) — to enlarge images without losing quality. Unlike standard interpolation, AI reconstructs missing pixels to create sharp, high-resolution versions of low-res originals.

### How AI Image Upscaling Works
Traditional resizing uses algorithms like Bicubic or Lanczos interpolation. These methods calculate average colors to fill gaps between pixels. The result is almost always a blurry image because the algorithm doesn't understand what a face, text, or texture should look like.

Our AI image upscaler uses Real-ESRGAN, a model trained on millions of low-resolution and high-resolution image pairs. It recognizes patterns, textures, and edges. When you upload a blurry photo, the AI fills in realistic detail — effectively sharpening blurry images and reconstructing the information that was lost.
