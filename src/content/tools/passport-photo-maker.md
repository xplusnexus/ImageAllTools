Passport and visa photo requirements are strict and vary by country — exact pixel dimensions, a plain white or off-white background, no shadows, neutral expression, and a specific file-size cap. Getting any one of these wrong is a common cause of rejected applications.

Upload a headshot, select your country's requirement preset, and the tool crops to the correct dimensions, helps normalize the background, and lets you compress to the required file size in one flow.
